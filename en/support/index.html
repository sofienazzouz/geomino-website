Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Explore on every map, all the games on the regions of France (except puzzle and against the clock), "Find it!" and "Which shape?" for the departments of Île-de-France and Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes, one player and the first six stickers. No ads.

What does Geomino Complete unlock?

Everything else, for a one-time payment of €2.99, no subscription: all 101 departments in every game, the countries of Europe, rivers and mountains, Spain, Italy and Germany, puzzle and against the clock on every map, up to 4 players and all stickers. The purchase is shared with your family (Family Sharing).

I already bought it on another device.

Open Settings (the gear at the top right, after the parental gate) and tap "Restore purchases". Use the same Apple Account as for the purchase.

How do I pass the parental gate?

It asks for the result of a multiplication written in words (for example "seven times eight" = 56), typed on the keypad. It stops a young child from opening the settings or the shop by accident.

How do I change the language?

In Settings › Language: French, English, Spanish, Italian or German, or "Automatic" to follow the device language. The change is immediate.

The voice does not speak.

Check that "Read aloud" is on in Geomino's settings and that the volume is up. If a language has no voice, add one in iPhone Settings › Accessibility › Spoken Content › Voices.

How do I add a player or reset progress?

Tap the profile on the home screen to switch player or add one (up to 4 with Geomino Complete). To reset or delete a player: Settings › Players › "…" button.
