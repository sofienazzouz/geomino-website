Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short
  • Geomino collects no data: not about children, not about parents.
  • No account, no server, no ads, no trackers, no analytics.
  • Players' progress stays on the device.

1. Who we are

The Geomino app is published by Sofien Azzouz ("we"). For any question about this policy: gymindconsulting@gmail.com.

2. What the app stores, on the device only

  • player profiles: a first name or nickname chosen freely, and an animal (avatar);
  • progress: stars, best scores, stickers earned, and the places often missed (so they are asked again more often);
  • settings: difficulty, language, appearance, read aloud, vibrations.

This information is only used to run the game. It is never sent to a server: Geomino has none, and we cannot access it. A real first name is not needed: a nickname is enough.

3. iPhone and iPad permissions

Geomino asks for no permission: no camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts or notifications. Read aloud uses the device's built-in speech, without any connection.

4. Advertising, analytics and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics, no trackers and no third-party SDK. We do not sell or share any data. If you agreed to it in your device settings, Apple may send us anonymous crash reports; they contain no information about players.

5. Children

Geomino is made for children aged 6 to 12 and follows the rules of the App Store Kids category. We collect no personal data from children. Settings, the shop and any link to the outside are protected by a parental gate (a multiplication written in words).

6. Purchase

Geomino Complete is a one-time purchase, with no subscription, fully handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details or your identity: the app only checks with Apple that the purchase was made. This is the only time the app contacts an online service (Apple's App Store).

7. When you write to us

If you contact us by email, we only use your address and message to reply, and delete them once the conversation is over (at the latest after 3 years). Please do not include information about your child: we do not need it.

8. Retention and deletion

Data stays on the device until you delete it. In Settings (parents) you can reset a player's progress or delete the player. Deleting the app erases everything. The device's iCloud backups, if turned on, may include this data depending on your Apple settings.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the rights of access, rectification, erasure and portability. As the data is only on your device, you exercise them directly in the app. For emails you sent us, write to us. You may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority (in France, the CNIL: www.cnil.fr).

10. This website

This website is a static page with no cookies, no forms, no external fonts and no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which may process technical connection data (such as the IP address) to provide and secure the site.

11. Changes

If the way the app works changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
